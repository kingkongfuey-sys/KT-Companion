Victory Points

Keep track of how many enemy operatives friendly operatives incapacitate during each turning point. Enemy operatives with a Wounds stat of 12 or more are treated as two for this purpose, and enemy operatives that are not incapacitated by a friendly operative are ignored (e.g., if they were incapacitated by the Hot weapon rule).

At the end of each turning point after the first, rank each player that incapacitated enemy operatives during that turning point. You score a number of VP as shown on the table opposite.

If players are tied on a rank, they share the rank at the higher end of that tie. For example, if four players incapacitated enemy operatives during the turning point, and two of them incapacitated the second most, they would both be ranked second. The remaining player would be ranked fourth.

How it's calculated
  • Exact probabilities, not a simulation. The target always rolls 3 defence dice and allocates saves to take the least damage.
  • Re-rolls are only used on failed dice. Ceaseless re-rolls the most common failed result.
  • Accurate dice count as normal successes and are not rolled.
  • Obscured: crits become normal successes, one success is discarded, and Rending, Severe and Punishing don't trigger.
  • A cover save uses one of the defence dice, so Piercing can remove it. Saturate removes cover saves.
  • Devastating damage can't be blocked and isn't changed by the damage modifiers. Damage never goes below 0.
  • Hot damage is dealt to the shooter and isn't part of the total.

Fight calculation
  • Both weapons roll their Attack characteristic at the same time.
  • Rerolls are single-use per die: a die rerolled once is not rerolled again.
  • The attacker normally resolves first. Optional ‘Strike twice’ and ‘pre-block’ rules can change the order shown above.
  • Normal Block removes an opponent's normal success; a critical Block can remove a normal or critical success.
  • The result is an exact probability calculation over all attack-dice outcomes; it is not a simulation.
